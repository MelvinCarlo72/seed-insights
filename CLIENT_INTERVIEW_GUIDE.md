# Purple Seedlings Child Profile Requirements Guide

**Purpose:** Confirm the company’s approved requirements for a staff-only child profile system before real information is entered. Use synthetic examples during prototype demonstrations. Do not record real names, contact information, developmental details, or session notes in interview notes.

## Profile requirements

1. Which basic child fields are required? Is date of birth needed, or would a less identifying age range be enough?
2. Should the profile support more than one parent or guardian contact?
3. Which service names should be available, and who maintains that list?
4. What does “developmental details” include? Are goals/focus areas, session history, and staff notes all approved? Which information must never be stored?
5. Do any of these fields contain clinical, assessment, treatment, or other specially sensitive information?

## Access and handling

6. Which job roles may view, create, edit, export, or delete profiles?
7. Who approves and removes staff accounts?
8. What consent, notice, retention, correction, deletion, and incident processes apply?
9. Are branch-level access limits required, or may every approved staff user see every profile?
10. What audit trail is required for viewing or changing child records?
11. Does the company approve AI-generated draft summaries from session-history notes? What kinds of notes are allowed, and what review or audit steps are required?
