# Purple Seedlings — Client Discovery Interview Guide

**Purpose:** Validate whether a parent/guardian feedback analytics tool addresses a real need. This guide is for an authorized center representative. Ask permission before taking notes or recording. Do not request child-identifying, diagnosis, assessment, or therapy information.

1. How does the center currently receive parent/guardian feedback?
2. Who reviews it, and how are recurring concerns identified or followed up?
3. Are there any difficulties with the current process? Please describe an example without sharing personal or child-specific details.
4. Which general service-experience areas are appropriate to ask about (communication, scheduling, staff interaction, facilities, parent support, or others)?
5. Would a voluntary QR feedback form be acceptable? Where could it be displayed?
6. Should feedback include a branch field? What are the approved branch names?
7. Who should be allowed to view raw comments versus aggregated reports?
8. What privacy notice, consent wording, retention period, and deletion process must be followed?
9. Are there existing systems or policies this project must integrate with or avoid duplicating?
10. What would make a prototype genuinely useful to the center?
